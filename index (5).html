One home base, four ways to use it

Families mix and match — daily afterschool care, full daycare for littles, à la carte enrichment classes, or full-day camps when school's out.

Grades K–5

Afterschool Program

We pick up from partner schools (or greet a parent drop-off) and take it from there — snack, focused homework time, an enrichment rotation, and free play, every single day.

  • School pickup & walking escort included
  • Dedicated quiet homework block
  • Runs until 6:30pm, snack included
Ages 2–5

Full & Half-Day Daycare

For the little ones not yet in kindergarten. A steady daily rhythm of learning centers, story time, outdoor play, rest, and meals — led by caregivers your child gets to know well.

  • Full-day or half-day scheduling
  • Meals, rest time & daily activity log
  • Consistent, familiar caregivers
All ages, drop-in or package

Co-Curricular Enrichment

Standalone classes any family can add on — no daily enrollment required. Rotating options include art, coding & robotics, music, chess, public speaking, and language classes, taught by specialists.

  • Drop-in or term-based packages
  • Small class sizes, real instructors
  • Open to kids outside our daily program
Teacher days & school breaks

Holiday & School-Break Camps

When school is closed but work isn't, we run full themed camp days — field-trip style outings, big art projects, and games, from morning drop-off through evening pickup.

  • Covers teacher in-service days
  • Winter, spring & summer break weeks
  • Full-day, 7:30am–6:30pm
